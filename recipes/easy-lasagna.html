Easy Lasagna

Description:

This recipe is easy to make and takes around an hour to cook. Serves about 10-12 people.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb. ground beef
  • 3 cups spaghetti sauce
  • 1 1/2 cups water
  • 1 3/4 cups ricotta
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese, divided
  • 1/2 cup grated parmesan cheese
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh parsley
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 9 peices lasagna, uncooked

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees.
  2. Brown mean over medium-high heat; drain. Add spaghetti sauce and water. Simmer for 10 minutes.
  3. In medium bowl, stir together ricotta cheese, one-half mozzarella cheese, parmesan cheese, eggs, parsley, salt, and pepper.
  4. Pour 1 cup sauce on bottom of 13 x 9 baking dish. Arrange 3 uncooked pasta pieces lengthwise over sauce; cover with 1 cup sauce. Spread one-half cheese filling over sauce. Repeat layers of lasagna, sauce, and cheese filling. Top with layer of lasagna and remaining sauce; sprinkle with mozzarella cheese.
  5. Cover with foil. Bake 45 minutes. Remove foil; bake an additional 10 mintues or until hot and bubbly.
  6. Let stand for 10 mintues. Serve.
